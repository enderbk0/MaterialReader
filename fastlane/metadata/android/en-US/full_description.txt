Open a PDF. Read it. That's it.

MaterialReader is a free and open-source PDF reader focused on making
reading PDFs simple and pleasant. There are no accounts, no subscriptions,
no advertisements, no analytics, and no cloud services. Reading a PDF never
requires paying money, creating an account, watching an advertisement, or
uploading the document.

Library features:
- Recent and favorite PDFs, folders, search, and sorting
- System document picker with persisted access
- Missing-file detection with one-tap relink

Reader features:
- Smooth scrolling with pinch, double-tap, and button zoom
- Offline text search, selectable page text, copy, share, and links
- Night mode that preserves photographs
- Print, details, jump-to-page, and reading-position memory

Privacy:
- Zero permissions declared, including no internet permission
- Everything stays on your device
- Open source under the Apache License 2.0
